
# Bhapa Pitha Recipe

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1.5 cups date palm jaggery (gur)
- Grated coconut
- A pinch of salt

## Instructions
1. Mix the rice flour with a pinch of salt.
2. Prepare the jaggery and grated coconut.
3. Layer the rice flour, gur, and coconut.
4. Steam until cooked.
5. Serve warm.
Serving suggestion: Serve with grated coconut.
