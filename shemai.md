# Eid Shemai Recipe

## Ingredients

- 200 g vermicelli
- 1 litre milk
- Sugar to taste
- 3 cardamom pods
- Chopped nuts
- A little ghee

## Instructions

1. Heat ghee and lightly roast the vermicelli.
2. Boil the milk with cardamom.
3. Add sugar and vermicelli.
4. Simmer until the vermicelli becomes tender.
5. Garnish with chopped nuts and serve.
